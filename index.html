Location sharing

Share your phone's location with your Dot through your Mac. Stop sharing anytime. Nearby map data stays on your Mac until unused for 30 days and can reveal past areas.

When Dot needs it, your Mac sends route coordinates to FOSSGIS and terrain areas to AWS. Data sources and privacy

Your Mac must be awake and connected. Location sharing pauses when you exit Dot Link on the glasses and resumes when you reopen it with this setting on.

Connect / disconnect your Mac

Set up the relay on your Mac before pairing this iPhone. An iPhone alone cannot connect to Dot.

GitHub download URL for Mac

https://github.com/YusukeIt0/dot-link/releases/latest

Open this URL in a browser on your Mac to download Dot Link. If copying is unavailable, enter the displayed URL in your Mac browser.

  1. Install Dot Link on your Mac

    Open the URL above on your Mac. Install and open Dot Link, then follow its setup instructions.

  2. Set up Tailscale on your iPhone

    Tailscale securely connects your iPhone to your Mac. Search for Tailscale in the App Store on this iPhone and install it. Open Tailscale, sign in with the same account used on your Mac, and follow the prompts to connect. Both devices must be connected to the same Tailscale network.

  3. Create a pairing code

    In Dot Link on your Mac, choose Connect Even and copy the code.

  4. Paste the code below to connect

    Return to this app, paste the entire code below, and choose Connect. To disconnect this iPhone later, use Revoke this device in this section.

The pairing code is valid for two minutes and one use.

Your relay Mac must be awake and online.
