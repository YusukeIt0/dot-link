Weather context

Weather is a separate, default-off option for the selected Mac. Enabling location sharing, walking information or history does not enable weather. Read and accept the MET Norway service terms before enabling it.

Your Mac sends latitude and longitude rounded to 0.01 degrees to api.met.no over HTTPS, together with an identifying Dot Link version and public project address. The service also sees your Mac’s outgoing IP address. No name, conversation, phone identifier or location history is sent. Rounding reduces precision but is not anonymity.

Dot can request forecasts for a fresh shared location or another place explicitly requested in conversation. The latter does not show where you are. With a current location and an active Dot location subscription, weather changes can update the same context. Dot decides whether to speak. Weather, maps, terrain and history are optional evidence; unavailable data does not stop conversation.

Freshness and limitations

These are model forecasts, including the values valid near the present time, not measurements at your position. Temperature, humidity, wind and precipitation retain their times and units. Rain amounts apply to an interval. Missing values remain unknown. No feels-like temperature or safety advice is calculated locally. Global detail is approximately 9 km, with finer Nordic/Arctic coverage; terrain, coastlines and local showers may be unresolved.

Saved forecasts are re-evaluated as time passes and reused within the provider’s limits. The same rounded query waits at least three hours before another request, including after failure; all requests also share a one-minute minimum. A new region has its own cache entry. Availability and precision are not guaranteed.

Storage and stopping

The Mac keeps at most 16 rounded-position forecasts, which expire after 24 hours. A maintenance pass runs hourly while the service is active; expired forecasts are removed on the next request or startup too, so physical cleanup can lag expiry. Up to 64 hashed query keys and retry timestamps remain until the service-specified cooldown ends, potentially longer than 24 hours after extended backoff. Coarse-position hashes can be guessed and are not anonymous. It stores no raw phone fixes or place labels in this weather cache. An invalid cache is replaced with a 24-hour recovery pause. Disabling weather cancels pending work and removes weather from live context after the Mac receives the change; the bounded cache remains until expiry to preserve request limits. Consent stays on that Mac until disabled or the paired owner is revoked. Restart requires fresh phone data for current-location weather.

Stopping location sharing, app exit, disconnection or stale location prevents current-location weather use. Explicitly requested places remain available while separate weather consent is enabled. A failed stop is shown as unconfirmed: reconnect and check the setting. Stops cannot retract requests already received by the service or information already received by Dot. Answers can remain in ordinary conversation history; Dot’s own data handling is separate.
