Weather context

Weather is a separate, default-off option for the selected Mac. Enabling location sharing, walking information or history does not enable weather. Read and accept the MET Norway service terms before enabling it.

On compatible Macs, turning on weather also lets Dot use nearby rain estimates. If you already enabled forecasts, they continue as before. You can add the satellite source once in Weather settings whenever you choose.

Your Mac sends latitude and longitude rounded to 0.01 degrees to api.met.no over HTTPS, together with an identifying Dot Link version and public project address. The service also sees your Mac’s outgoing IP address. No name, conversation, phone identifier or location history is sent. Rounding reduces precision but is not anonymity.

Dot can request forecasts for a fresh shared location or another place explicitly requested in conversation. The latter does not show where you are. With a current location and an active Dot location subscription, weather changes can update the same context. Dot decides whether to speak. Weather, maps, terrain and history are optional evidence; unavailable data does not stop conversation.

Nearby rain estimates from NOAA

When Dot requests weather for a shared or explicitly requested place, a compatible Mac with your permission can download a global NOAA satellite file from Amazon Web Services (AWS). AWS receives your outgoing IP address, the requested public file or time window, and standard request headers. The Mac selects the area locally. AWS receives no coordinates, place names, identifiers or conversation content from Dot Link in this request.

NOAA’s Blended Enterprise Rainfall Rates estimate recent precipitation using satellite imagery. Dot Link summarizes usable grid cells within 5 km and in eight directions out to 20 km. The grid covers 60°S to 70°N. Its ten-minute editions can arrive late, cover only part of an area or have degraded quality. The summary gives the source interval and its availability on the Mac. The observation time at an individual point is unknown. A recent estimate cannot tell you whether or when rain will reach you.

The Mac caches up to three verified global files for reuse across places, with one additional file while checking a download. Requests share a ten-minute minimum interval and a rolling download budget of 64 MiB per 24 hours. Live reads use recent editions for up to 45 minutes. Global files can remain on disk after that until cache replacement. Summaries for up to eight rounded places stay temporarily in memory. Missing satellite data leaves the forecast available.

Forecast freshness and limitations

These are model forecasts, including the values valid near the present time, not measurements at your position. Temperature, humidity, wind and precipitation retain their times and units. Rain amounts apply to an interval. Missing values remain unknown. No feels-like temperature or safety advice is calculated locally. Global detail is approximately 9 km, with finer Nordic/Arctic coverage; terrain, coastlines and local showers may be unresolved.

Saved forecasts are re-evaluated as time passes and reused within the provider’s limits. The same rounded query waits at least three hours before another request, including after failure; all requests also share a one-minute minimum. A new region has its own cache entry. Availability and precision vary.

Storage and stopping

The Mac keeps at most 16 rounded-position forecasts, which expire after 24 hours. A maintenance pass runs hourly while the service is active; expired forecasts are removed on the next request or startup too, so physical cleanup can lag expiry. Up to 64 hashed query keys and retry timestamps remain until the service-specified cooldown ends, potentially longer than 24 hours after extended backoff. Coarse-position hashes can be guessed and are not anonymous. It stores no raw phone fixes or place labels in this weather cache. An invalid cache is replaced with a 24-hour recovery pause. Disabling weather cancels pending work and removes weather from live context after the Mac receives the change; the bounded cache remains until expiry to preserve request limits. Consent stays on that Mac until disabled or the paired owner is revoked. Restart requires fresh phone data for current-location weather.

Stopping location sharing, app exit, disconnection or stale location prevents current-location weather use. Explicitly requested places remain available while separate weather consent is enabled. A failed stop is shown as unconfirmed: reconnect and check the setting. Stops cannot retract requests already received by the service or information already received by Dot. Answers can remain in ordinary conversation history; Dot’s own data handling is separate.

Weather history starts off. If you enable it separately, your Mac encrypts the returned forecast values, place and revision times for your selected retention period. If you also enable nearby rain estimates, the same history setting saves regional satellite summaries, the shared or requested place, the source interval and when each edition became available on this Mac. Only new eligible evidence after both choices can enter that history. Dot can use those times to look up what was available earlier. History makes no extra provider requests. You can stop or delete it separately from the live weather service.

Source and license

Based on data from MET Norway, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Dot Link rounds query coordinates, normalizes units and selects a subset of forecast fields. Source credit is also included in tool results.

Satellite estimates come from NOAA/NESDIS Blended Enterprise Rainfall Rates, distributed through the NOAA Open Data Dissemination program on AWS.
