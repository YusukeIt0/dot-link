Before you start

What you’ll need.

  • Apple Silicon MacKept awake and online while you use Dot Link.
  • Even G2 + iPhoneWith the Even Realities app on your phone.
  • TailscaleOn Mac and iPhone, signed in to the same account.
  • Your existing ChatGPT DotWith access to OpenAI tunnels and custom MCP connections.

Dot Link does not provide access to these ChatGPT features. Check the current requirements before installing.

Get connected

From Mac to glasses.

  1. Install Dot Link on your Mac.
  2. Connect your existing Dot.
  3. Open Dot Link in Even Hub and pair your devices.
