# Data handling

Implementation baseline: Mac 0.4.14 and Even 0.3.29 source, reviewed October 24, 2026. Photo attachments additionally reflect the camera/photo implementation branch (Mac 0.4.18 and Even 0.3.38); physical acceptance and distribution are pending. This baseline describes implementation, not Even Hub publication status. Location sharing requires compatible versions on both devices; legacy-version differences are noted below.

[Quickstart](https://github.com/YusukeIt0/dot-link#quickstart) · [User guide](setup.md)

Dot Link connects Even G2 to your existing ChatGPT Dot through a relay on a Mac you control. There is no shared conversation server operated by the developer. Your Mac and iPhone need your own Tailscale connection.

## What is collected and sent

- **Audio and conversations:** Hold-to-talk captures G2 audio until release or a stop action, with a 30-second limit. “Disable hold-to-record” blocks manual recording from the glasses and phone; enabling it discards an active manual recording without sending it for transcription. Text and separately enabled wake input remain available. Optional Hey dot input (default off) keeps the G2 microphone open while Dot Link is active and detects the cue locally on the phone. Waiting audio stays in bounded memory and is not sent to a service or written to a recording file. Following speech is captured for up to 30 seconds and ends on silence. OFF, unpairing and app exit stop listening; a transient connection failure pauses new activation without discarding an active utterance; read-aloud and processing pause it. See [wake-word behavior and validation limits](wake-word.md). Audio goes over HTTPS to your Mac. Free local Whisper transcription is the default. If you explicitly select an external service (Soniox, ElevenLabs, Microsoft, OpenAI or AssemblyAI) in Speech input on your Mac, subsequent recordings go directly from your Mac to that service using your API key. Your service account charges and limits apply. Unselected services are not contacted, and failures do not trigger an automatic provider switch. Selecting OpenAI transcription is separate from connecting your existing Dot. Transcribed or manually submitted text goes to your existing Dot through your personal ChatGPT connection. Replies and proactive messages return through the Mac to G2.
- **Pairing and camera:** The current pairing flow pastes a code generated on the Mac. It does not use the camera. Legacy Even packages (0.2.13 and below) and the web UI bundled with Mac 0.2.21 contain hidden QR/camera code. Pairing codes are credentials; do not share them.
- **Head movement:** With “Show when I raise my head” enabled, G2 motion sensor values are used locally to decide when to restore the display. This setting defaults to on and can be disabled in display settings. Dot Link has no code to send these sensor values to the Mac or Dot, or store a movement history. Settings such as the calibrated forward angle are saved.
- **Mac notifications:** This optional prototype reads Notification Center title, description and value strings through macOS Accessibility while monitoring is active. Ordinary application notification strings may be read into memory for classification. With “Share all Mac notifications with Dot” enabled, visible sender names, headings and text from readable new notifications go through the Mac relay to your existing Dot. Sharing defaults to off. Notifications may be shared without an identified source; unknown sources and senders are not inferred. Chats and emails are not opened to retrieve content beyond the notification. Dot decides whether and when to announce it. Not every notification format or immediate arrival is guaranteed, and replying in the source app is not supported. Pausing notifications alone does not stop the conversation relay.
- **Read aloud:** When enabled, Dot replies are passed to the device's Web Speech API. Whether speech processing stays on the device depends on the device and voice engine.

## Photos

Camera and photo-library access occurs only when you tap the corresponding button in the phone composer. The Even SDK currently selects one image at a time; you can add up to four to a draft. Dot Link re-encodes the pixels as JPEG, with a maximum edge of 2,048 pixels and a limit of 512 KiB per photo. Original file names, device paths and EXIF metadata (including GPS) are not forwarded. Photos may still visibly contain private information.

Draft photos upload to your paired Mac when you tap **Send**, or when voice transcription completes with automatic speech sending enabled. Selecting or capturing a photo alone does not upload it. The Mac sends a text notification to your existing Dot; image bodies are available through the authenticated pending-message tool. Photo bodies are not included in webhook notifications, device history, status responses or timing logs. No separate image-analysis provider is used.

Draft photos remain in page memory, not Dot Link browser storage. Stopping or disconnecting clears them; reopening does not restore them. The Mac stores accepted photos in owner-only local files referenced by its conversation state until Dot replies, 24 hours have elapsed since sending, or the conversation is evicted from the latest 100 messages. Expired photos are marked unavailable; resend them if needed. The running relay cleans them up automatically, or on restart if it was stopped. Pending photos are limited to 64 MiB in total; a full queue rejects new photos until capacity is freed. After a reply, the retained conversation includes the photo count and digests for retry detection, not the photo bodies. This does not remove copies already received by Dot or included in system backups.

## What is stored

**On the Mac:** With Whisper, recordings and transcription output are temporary working files, removed on normal completion or processing errors. Automatic cleanup after power loss or forced termination is not implemented. Up to 100 conversations, 100 notifications and 1,000 proactive messages are stored. Proactive messages (including location announcements) rotate in acceptance order: each new message beyond 1,000 replaces the oldest stored message. Identical retries of retained IDs do not add a message or refresh its position; conflicting text for a retained ID is rejected. Once a record is removed, its ID and text are no longer available for duplicate or conflict checks, so that ID can be accepted again as a new message. These histories are not automatically deleted by age. Mac history and Dot's own memory and history are separate.

External STT paths send audio and receive transcription in memory without creating recording files. The provider choice is stored in local Mac settings; each service API key is stored in macOS Keychain. You can save, replace and delete it in Speech input. Keychain permission dialogs are never shown during transcription. If access fails, you are directed to explicitly allow access in settings. App updates may require permission again.

The Mac also stores connection and tunnel settings, OpenAI tunnel credentials, relay authentication keys, and event delivery destinations and secrets. These are local files; files containing secrets are given owner-only read/write permissions. They are not all hashed or stored in Keychain. The all-notification sharing choice, paused state, language and update preferences are stored as Mac app settings.

**On the Even side:** WebView storage and the Even SDK's app storage retain the relay address, session credential, expiry, app origin and update-recovery credential. The optional voice-activation choice, custom wake name, hold-to-record disablement and read-aloud choice are saved. Changing the name does not enable the microphone. Language and display preferences are also saved: idle time, wake gesture, head-raise enablement, angle and calibrated forward angle. Disconnecting retains a separate pending-revocation record containing the old relay address, credentials and expiry until acknowledgment or expiry. It is used only to remove access, never to restore a session, and survives a new pairing. Old origin-local caches can remain until revisited or cleared. Dot Link does not itself implement Keychain storage for these records.

An uncertain outgoing message has a saved ID and SHA-256 content digest (text and any attached photos) for duplicate prevention. This record does not contain the message text. Short-text hashes may be guessable and are not treated as anonymous data.

**Pairing expiry:** Codes last two minutes and can be used once. Sessions last at most 90 days; recovery does not extend the original expiry. Mac pairing records retain hashes of session and recovery credentials, origins and expiry times, without the original tokens. To prevent old app instances from taking over a reopened connection, Mac recovery records also store the current runtime ID, origin, ownership generation and a random nonce. These values do not grant access without the recovery credential. Individual revocation atomically marks the registration inactive; hashed revocation receipts remain until expiry and subsequent pruning so retries cannot restore access. The recovery credential can revoke only its own registration from a packaged app, without recovering a data session. This describes pairing records, not the separate tunnel and other credentials listed above.

## Diagnostics and external services

A failed Mac relay activation can save its last failed step, a known error code, available command exit/signal information and time in an owner-only local diagnostic file. It is replaced by a later failure and removed after activation completes without an error when file permissions allow. **Copy diagnostics** copies the current error details and app version/build, excluding credentials, hostnames, paths, command output and conversations. Neither the file nor the copied text is uploaded automatically.

Timing logs contain processing IDs, stages, timestamps and durations. Notification diagnostics store permission and monitoring states, the sharing setting, legacy selected app names, counts, synthetic test notification bodies, and detection and forwarding results. Ordinary notification strings are handled in memory for classification and deduplication; their bodies are not stored in the notification diagnostic file or delivery ledger. The ledger stores up to 4,096 notification-identifier hashes, delivery IDs, timestamps, stages and attempt counts. Shared notification text is stored in the relay notification history and sent to Dot. These diagnostic files are not automatically sent to the developer. Review their contents before sharing them for support.

Dot Link has no app analytics or advertising SDK. The separate official website offers optional Google Analytics, described below. Initial speech-model downloads connect to Hugging Face; update feeds and signed archives come from GitHub. Those hosts receive ordinary connection information such as IP addresses. Dot Link does not attach conversations, recordings, pairing credentials or tunnel credentials to those download requests. Updates require no GitHub token, and Sparkle system-profile reporting is disabled.

Conversations sent to OpenAI, and information handled by the OS, Even and Tailscale, are also subject to those services' rules and settings. This document describes Dot Link's implementation; it does not guarantee storage or deletion behavior inside external services.

When Soniox is selected, its [data handling policy](https://soniox.com/docs/security-and-privacy) and your agreement apply. Dot Link cannot guarantee storage or deletion within Soniox.

The Microsoft endpoint is stored in local Mac settings. Review the official pricing and data handling links in Speech input and your agreement with the selected service. Dot Link does not guarantee provider-side retention or deletion.

## Disconnecting and deleting

- **“Revoke this device” on the Even side:** Immediately stops this device’s active connection and saves disconnect intent separately from ordinary pairing. While Dot Link is running, it retries Mac-side removal after network failures and again on reopening, without restoring the disconnected registration. A pending message distinguishes local stop from confirmed Mac removal. If native storage fails, the app reports that reopening may restore the connection; keep it open to retry or remove the connections on the Mac. It does not erase Mac conversation history or all Even-side language, display and duplicate-prevention records. Deletion through the Even SDK can also fail; Mac-side revocation is needed to reliably invalidate access.
- **Removing Even connections on the Mac:** Open **Even connection settings → Remove all Even connections…**. After confirmation, this invalidates all connections registered with this Mac's pairing codes, their recovery credentials, and unused codes. Location context from those connections is no longer usable. It works while the relay is paused or offline; each phone shows the change when it next reaches this Mac. Pair again with a new code to resume use. Saved-registration counts can include older registrations for the same phone and are not device counts. ChatGPT connection settings and conversation history are kept. The separate legacy development key is not a code-paired connection and is not rotated by this action. The maintenance command `node scripts/beta-pair.mjs revoke-all` uses the same revocation boundary.
- **Removing the Mac app:** Use “Uninstall Dot Link…” in the app. It stops the relay and notifications, removes login items, and moves the app, dedicated runtime, speech model, connection settings and conversation history to Trash. All service keys saved by Speech input are deleted from Keychain. If key deletion fails, removal does not proceed. There is no option to retain settings; using the app again requires setup. The older Mac 0.2.21 offered an option to retain settings and history.
- **Removal scope:** Files are moved to Trash, rather than immediately and permanently erased. Development installations remove known target files; custom files, previous backups and some older logs may remain. This does not erase device backups, Even-side storage, update credentials stored in Keychain by earlier development releases, or data held by external services. Mac display and notification preferences are removed after a backup is moved to Trash; if that backup fails, the completion dialog says the preferences were kept.

Stopping services alone does not delete history. Manage or delete information already sent to OpenAI or other services separately with those services.

## Location sharing

Location sharing starts off. When enabled in the Even app, the phone location, measurement time and accuracy (and speed/heading when available) are sent to your connected Mac. Your existing Dot can read the latest location and decide whether to offer useful information. Turning sharing off stops new location uploads and requests that the Mac clear its current location and block location-based announcements. If the stop cannot reach the Mac, that remote state is not cleared immediately: sharing expires 90 seconds after the last accepted relay update, and old measurements cannot be used as fresh location. Pending stop requests are saved and retried. A confirmed glasses-app exit also ends the live sharing session while retaining the saved choice for the next launch. Hiding the phone page alone does not stop sharing. You control the OS and Even app location permissions.

Dot Link does not save a coordinate trail to disk. The latest location and short-lived decision context remain in Mac memory; preferences, subscriptions and revoked identifiers are saved. Dot's announcements enter ordinary conversation history and may contain place names. OpenAI's handling of locations read by Dot depends on your agreement and settings. Turning sharing off does not delete information already sent. Background operation, actual update intervals and battery impact depend on the device and OS.

## Official website analytics

The [official website](https://yusukeit0.github.io/dot-link/) and its language versions offer optional Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and improve the introduction and installation links. This measures the website only. It does not add analytics to the Mac app, Even app, installer, conversations or relay.

**Your choice:** Google Analytics is off until you select **Allow analytics**. Rejecting or leaving the choice unanswered does not affect reading the site, watching its video or following installation links. Before consent, the website does not load Google's analytics script or send analytics events, including cookieless analytics pings. Actions taken before consent are not replayed later. Open **Analytics settings** in the footer to change your choice. Selecting **Reject analytics** stops future collection and removes this site's accessible analytics cookies; the page reloads if the Google tag was already loaded. It does not erase data previously received by Google.

**What is sent:** After consent, Google receives page views, visits and engagement, the page language, a 90% scroll event, outbound-link clicks, clicks on installation and other primary action links, language changes, and video start/end events. The site supplies fixed action/video identifiers and page language rather than conversation content or form input. An installation-link click means someone opened Even Hub, not that an app was installed. A video-end event means playback reached the end after a measured start, not proof that every second was watched.

The page URL sent by our configuration excludes its query string and fragment. Referral URLs are reduced to the referring origin, except for known official LP paths. Four public campaign labels (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) are accepted only as short lowercase letters/digits/underscore/hyphen values. Do not put personal information or secrets in campaign links. Google's tag also receives ordinary connection and browser/device information, including the IP address used to connect. Google states that GA4 uses IP addresses to derive approximate location and does not log or store individual IP addresses. These statistics and cookie identifiers are not described as fully anonymous.

**Storage:** The site stores your allow/reject choice and its expiry in browser local storage for 180 days, shared across these language pages. If that choice cannot be saved, analytics stays off. After consent, GA4 uses first-party cookies with the `dotlink` prefix, scoped to `/dot-link/`, with a configured maximum lifetime of 180 days and no extension on each visit. Browsers may expire them sooner. Clearing site data removes your choice and cookies. GA4 is configured for 14-month user/event data retention; this setting does not determine retention of Google's standard aggregated reports or backups. See [Google's data-retention explanation](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196).

**Internal visits:** Analytics settings can mark a browser as internal using a local true/false preference, without an IP-based rule or a personal identifier. With analytics consent, that browser's events carry `traffic_type=internal` for exclusion by the property's internal-traffic filter. This preference applies only to that browser/profile, persists until changed or site data is cleared, and must be set separately on another device. Denied consent still prevents sending, even for internal visits.

Google signals, advertising personalization and user-provided-data collection are not enabled for this measurement, and no advertising accounts are linked. Search Console is linked for analysis of search discovery; this does not add another visitor-side tag. Google processes the analytics data under its applicable terms and policies. See [how Google uses information from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and [GA4's IP-address handling](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/12017362). GitHub Pages also receives ordinary hosting requests regardless of the analytics choice.
